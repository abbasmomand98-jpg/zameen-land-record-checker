PLOT VERIFICATION

Check a plot before you pay

Enter a plot's file number. Zameen reads the record back in plain language: fard, owner chain, society NOC, stay orders and mortgages.

Try: ZM-2026-214 (safe), ZM-2026-077 (high risk), ZM-2026-310 (caution).

SOCIETY & NOC CHECKER

Is the society safe to buy in?

A valid society NOC is the first thing to check. An expired NOC means the society should not be selling new files at all.

TRUE COST CALCULATOR

What will it really cost?

Stamp duty 1%, registration 1%, CVT 2% of the DC rate. Know the full official amount before the registry office — no hidden fees.

Sample: a 10-marla plot at a DC rate of Rs 45 lakh costs Rs 1,80,000 in official charges. The official fard fee is Rs 500 — a demand of Rs 60,000 for it is a Rs 59,500 overcharge.
